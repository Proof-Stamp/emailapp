How it helps

One ProofStamp for one or more files.

01

Create file fingerprints

Your browser creates a unique SHA-256 fingerprint for each file. The files are never uploaded to ProofStamp.

02

Email one ProofStamp

One email keeps the fingerprints, your description, and the email received time together.

03

Check later

Verify one file or all of them. A matching fingerprint confirms that file has not changed.

What a ProofStamp does not prove

It does not prove when or where a file was originally created, who made it, whether it was edited before the ProofStamp, or whether its contents are true. The email received time records when the ProofStamp reached the inbox.
