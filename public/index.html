How it works

Your device creates the proof. Your email keeps it.

01

Create the file fingerprint

Your browser creates a SHA-256 hash, a unique fingerprint for the file. The file is not uploaded.

02

Keep the ProofStamp

Email it, copy it, or save it as a text file.

03

Check later

A matching fingerprint confirms the file has exactly the same contents.

What a ProofStamp does not prove

It does not prove when or where a file was created, who made it, whether it was edited before the ProofStamp, or whether its contents are true. The email received time shows when the ProofStamp reached that inbox.
